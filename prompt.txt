# Build a Church Worship Service Schedule Generator

I want to build a schedule generator for my church that assigns members to specific Sunday worship service duties based on church rules, rank, gender, age, and fairness rotation.

## Project Goal

Create a web-based schedule generator that accepts a list of church members and automatically produces Sunday service assignments for the following roles:

1. Service Conductor
2. Three Members Prayer
   - Prayer 1
   - Prayer 2
   - Prayer 3
3. Bible Readings
   - First Lesson Reader
   - Second Lesson Reader

The system should be built in a clean, maintainable way so I can add more church-specific rules later.

---

## Scheduling Rules

### 1. Service Conductor

The Service Conductor must normally be:

- Male
- Adult
- A man of rank
- Preferably higher rank

Exception:

- Every third Sunday of the month, the Service Conductor must be a young male between ages 11 and 18.

The system should detect whether a Sunday is the third Sunday of the month.

If it is the third Sunday:
- Select only eligible young males between 11 and 18.

If it is not the third Sunday:
- Select an eligible adult male of rank.

---

### 2. Three Members Prayer

There are three consecutive prayers.

#### Prayer 1
- Must be male
- Usually lower rank preferred

#### Prayer 2
- Must be female
- Any rank allowed

#### Prayer 3
- Must be male
- Preferably higher rank

The same person should not be assigned to more than one prayer on the same Sunday.

---

### 3. Bible Readings

There are two readings:

- First Lesson Reader
- Second Lesson Reader

Rules:

- Both readers must be male
- Can be lower or higher rank
- Preferably avoid assigning the same person to both readings
- Preferably avoid assigning someone already assigned to Service Conductor or Three Members Prayer that same Sunday, unless there are not enough available members

---

## Fairness Rules

The scheduler should try to rotate assignments fairly.

When selecting members:

- Prefer members who have not served recently
- Avoid assigning the same member too often
- Avoid assigning one person to multiple duties on the same Sunday
- Track assignment history
- Allow manual override after the schedule is generated

The selection should not be purely random. It should use weighted logic based on:

- Eligibility
- Last assigned date
- Number of recent assignments
- Role suitability
- Rank preference
- Availability

# Scheduler Logic

Create a scheduling engine with modular rule processing.

Scheduler flow:

Step 1:
Get all Sundays in selected date range

Step 2:
Determine Sunday type:

- Regular Sunday
- Third Sunday of month

Step 3:
Load church rules

Step 4:
Load all active members

Step 5:
Filter eligible candidates for each role

Examples:

Service Conductor:

Normal Sunday:

- Male
- Adult
- Rank required

Third Sunday:

- Male
- Age 11–18

Prayer 1:

- Male
- Lower rank preferred

Prayer 2:

- Female
- Any rank

Prayer 3:

- Male
- Higher rank preferred

Bible Readings:

- Male
- Lower or Higher rank

Step 6:

Calculate scores for all eligible members

Step 7:

Sort members by score

Step 8:

Assign highest-scoring member

Step 9:

Prevent duplicate assignments where possible

Step 10:

Store assignment history

Step 11:

Move to next Sunday

---

# Scoring Logic Example

Use weighted scoring instead of random selection.

Each eligible member receives a score.

Example:

Initial score:

0

Fairness scoring:

+50
Member has not served recently

+30
Member matches preferred rank

+20
Member has fewer assignments overall

+15
Member has not served within last two Sundays

+10
Member belongs to preferred age category

Penalty scoring:

-40
Member served previous Sunday

-50
Member already assigned another duty that day

-75
Member served same role recently

-100
Inactive member

-100
Unavailable member

-1000
Rule violation

Examples:

Prayer 1:

John:
Lower Rank
Not served in three weeks

Score:

+30
+50
+20

Total = 100

Prayer 3:

Michael:
Higher Rank
Served previous Sunday

Score:

+30
-40

Total = -10

Select highest scoring candidate.
